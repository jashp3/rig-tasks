Tasks

Thesis: still a pressure valve — capture in three seconds, never decide at capture, filing happens later. What changed is the two places plain Inbox was weakest: today is now the day itself, hour by hour down the page, and the inbox no longer quietly rots, because whatever has been waiting longest is pinned where you cannot miss it.

Kept — Inbox context field
“Add what you know”, timestamped lines. This was already in Inbox; it needed nothing.
Kept, changed — from Now
An optional time of day, and the day itself as an hour-by-hour vertical run. A map of when things are, never how long — we do not model duration, so no block heights lie about it.
Cut — from Now
The tray, and “no time in mind” as a rule. The tray is just Inbox’s own tab wearing a different hat. Tap-to-create on the clock is not built: it needs a duration and an auto-placed slot, and both have already been ruled out.
Kept, adapted — from Threads
Carried longest: the oldest thing in the inbox is pinned to the top, with how long you have been carrying it.
Cut — from Threads
“Add lines while you think”. That is the context field again. Building it twice would have been two names for one thing.

Times are entirely optional and off by default — filing stays four taps and no clock. Type a time into the capture box (“dentist tomorrow 4pm”) and it is picked up for you. Today then shows the day as hours down the page; a task with no time stays off the clock and says so. Press Play the 3 flows to watch it run.
