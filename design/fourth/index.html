Direction 4 — Inbox, plus the two things that survived

Thesis: still a pressure valve — capture in three seconds, never decide at capture, filing happens later. What changes is the two places plain Inbox was weakest: today now has a visible shape and a closing feeling instead of a flat list, and the inbox no longer quietly rots, because whatever has been waiting longest is pinned where you cannot miss it.

Kept — Inbox context field
“Add what you know”, timestamped lines. This was already in Inbox; it needed nothing.
Kept, reduced — from Now
An optional time of day, and a day bar that shows when your things are. A map, not a duration — we do not model how long anything takes.
Cut — from Now
The tray, the hourly grid, and “no time in mind” as a rule. The tray is just Inbox's own tab wearing a different hat.
Kept, adapted — from Threads
Carried longest: the oldest thing in the inbox is pinned to the top, with how long you have been carrying it.
Cut — from Threads
“Add lines while you think”. That is the context field again. Building it twice would have been two names for one thing.

Times are entirely optional and off by default — filing stays four taps and no clock. Type a time into the capture box (“dentist tomorrow 4pm”) and it is picked up for you. Tap any marker on the day bar to move that task. Press Play the 3 flows to watch it run.
