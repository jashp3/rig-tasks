Direction 2 — Now

Thesis: a task manager is the day you are currently having, drawn as a clock you carry — anything not placed in time is not a task yet, it is a wish. It optimises for answering “what am I doing right now” in under a second and for ending the day with a visible, closed day — and at the expense of every open loop that has no natural time: those get pushed into a tray and, honestly, ignored.

The clock is the structure: tap any empty hour on the phone to put something there, tap the dot on a block to close it out. The phone carries one day plus an unscheduled tray; the laptop puts two days side by side and turns the tray into buttons. Both views share the same state.
