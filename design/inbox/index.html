Direction 1 — Inbox

Thesis: a personal task manager is a pressure valve: the fastest possible path from “I must not forget this” to “it is out of my head and safe in here”. It optimises for capture in under three seconds and for triage that never blocks you — and at the expense of never telling you what to do right now, which you have to derive yourself from the day you file it into.

Every screen is live and clickable. The Both view runs the same state on a phone and a desktop side by side — tap in one, watch the other. Play the 3 flows drives capture → today → complete automatically. Try opening these files on your phone: they are the same files.
