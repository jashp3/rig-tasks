# Tasks

A personal task manager. The bar is not "feature complete" — it is **"the
owner opens this daily and prefers it to whatever they used before."**

Optimise for capture speed, a calm reading experience, and a complete sense
of what is done and what is not. Everything that does not serve those three is
out of scope until proven otherwise.

## Success condition

The owner can capture a task in one keystroke, see everything outstanding at a
glance, and close something out — without the app ever feeling heavy or
asking more of them than the task itself does.
