Four directions

Three structurally different takes on the same app, plus a synthesis of the pieces worth keeping. Open one - it is live, not a picture. Switch between Phone and Desktop, and press Play the 3 flows to watch capture, today, and complete run.

1 - Inbox

capture-first triage

Gives up: it never tells you what to do now.

2 - Now

clock / day timeline

Gives up: open loops get trayed and ignored.

3 - Threads

no lists, no dates, no taxonomy

Gives up: you never see the shape of a week.

4 - Synthesis

Inbox, plus the two borrowed ideas that survived

Adds: an optional time of day and a day bar, plus carried longest pinned to the top.
Nothing here is decided yet. The pick comes after you have used them.
